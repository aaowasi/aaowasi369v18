Decision boundary

Evidence checks can pass while legal applicability or marking effectiveness remains unresolved. Approved lifecycle changes require human review.

Review procedure

  1. Establish system scope, owner, observation period and evidence provenance.
  2. Execute the applicable check and inspect unknown, stale or failed results.
  3. Record an evidence-linked finding, assign a treatment owner and set a review date.
  4. Retest with new evidence. Keep the earlier snapshot and the reviewer’s conclusion.
